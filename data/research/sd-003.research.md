## Primary technical sources

1. Apache ZooKeeper project, *ZooKeeper Recipes and Solutions*, version 3.8.4. The lock recipe supplies the ephemeral sequential node protocol, attempt GUID recovery after uncertain creation, immediate-predecessor watching and shared-lock variations. Chapters 2 and 3 trace new concrete executions of these mechanisms. https://zookeeper.apache.org/doc/r3.8.4/recipes.html

2. PostgreSQL Global Development Group, *Explicit Locking*, PostgreSQL 19, section 13.3. Provides documented transaction-versus-session advisory-lock lifetimes and deadlock guidance. Chapters 4 and 5 connect these semantics to connection pooling and ordered acquisition. https://www.postgresql.org/docs/19/explicit-locking.html

3. etcd project, *API reference: concurrency*, v3.4. Ownership keys may be used with transactions to guard changes to etcd state. Chapter 4 examines why an unprotected external service falls outside that transaction. https://etcd.io/docs/v3.4/dev-guide/api_concurrency_reference_v3/

4. Martin Kleppmann, *How to do distributed locking*, February 8, 2016. Individual technical analysis of efficiency locks, correctness locks and delayed clients. Used for the distinction in chapter 1 and the stale write in chapter 4. https://martin.kleppmann.com/2016/02/08/how-to-do-distributed-locking.html

## Google

5. Mike Burrows, *The Chubby Lock Service for Loosely-Coupled Distributed Systems*, OSDI 2006. Historical production evidence for coarse-grained locks, sequencers and the responsibilities of lock clients and protected services. https://www.usenix.org/legacy/event/osdi06/tech/full_papers/burrows/burrows_html/

Original practice: locate the exact resource that rejects a delayed write. Defend a same-store transaction against a separate coordinator and provider API. The availability cost differs substantially when the provider cannot settle an uncertain result.

## OpenAI

6. OpenAI, *Scaling Kubernetes to 7,500 nodes*, January 25, 2021. The engineering publication establishes a large research-cluster context and control-plane scaling concerns. https://openai.com/index/scaling-kubernetes-to-7500-nodes/

Original practice: use a cold model rollout to compare fan-out to every worker, one materializer per zone, and chunk-level parallel transfer. Estimate traffic saved and identify the small metadata transition that actually requires exclusion.

## Meta

8. Mahesh Balakrishnan and colleagues, *Virtual Consensus in Delos*, OSDI 2020. Documents a Facebook replicated-log architecture and how coordination infrastructure can evolve behind a logical interface. https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi20/presentation/balakrishnan

Original practice: discuss how a service would migrate the authority for a hot key without independently granting incompatible ownership on two shards. The proposed lock queue and artifact service are hypothetical.

## NVIDIA

9. NVIDIA, *Megatron-LM integration*, NVIDIA Resiliency Extension 0.7.0 documentation. Its separation of asynchronous checkpoint operations and completed recoverable state motivates the difference between staging data and publishing a durable pointer. https://nvidia.github.io/nvidia-resiliency-ext/fault_tolerance/integration/megatron.html

Original practice: explain why duplicate immutable work can be acceptable but publishing an incomplete manifest is a correctness failure. Quantify how a long-held lock affects recovery time under synchronized retries.
