## OpenAI — public database operations

The PostgreSQL article includes public schema-management and operational context. Original practice: budget locks, primary load, replication lag, and compatibility during change. The lesson's row counts and migration state machine are not reported OpenAI measurements or internal procedures.

## Anthropic — public workflow context and limits

The multi-agent research account discusses production workflow engineering. It does not disclose a universal deployment or approval protocol. Original practice: preserve run state, scoped approval, and effect identity across runtime changes; drain or explicitly migrate workflows that cannot safely resume under a new version.

## Meta — historical release engineering

The rapid-release publication provides historical context for safe incremental change at scale. It does not establish current private release thresholds. Original practice: distinguish deployment, release, and durable migration; defend cohort selection and legal intermediate states rather than copying an assumed company policy.

## Google — canary reasoning

The SRE workbook grounds canary evaluation as a release-engineering technique. Our error counts, rough independent Bernoulli calculation, and stop policy are original exercises. They require stated cohort, correlation, and observation assumptions; they are not a universal statistical promotion algorithm.

## NVIDIA — lifecycle versus product quality

Triton documents model-management behavior. Original practice: separate repository presence, loading, readiness, admission, and customer-quality evaluation. A versioned compatibility bundle and behavioral guardrails are proposed product architecture, not features attributed wholesale to Triton.

## Supporting product-specific evidence

Kubernetes grounds rolling-update availability and surge concepts, with lifecycle and termination caveats. PostgreSQL grounds version-specific schema operations and constraint validation. gh-ost provides a concrete MySQL online-migration reference. No source justifies treating all operations called online as lock-free or universally reversible.

## Staff+ rehearsal and validation limits

Defend a rollout under zone loss, warmup contention, missing telemetry, cohort imbalance, stale backfill, delayed delete, old paused writer, long stream, and lost external-effect acknowledgment. Identify the precise rollback boundary and a compatible forward mitigation. The executable model validates local preconditions and arithmetic only, not a production orchestrator or statistical test. Every question is original practice.
