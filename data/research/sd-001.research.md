# SD-001 · How a failover decision survives a crash

## How to read the Payments example

Payments normally runs in Ashburn. Phoenix is the standby. Two operators ask to run the same failover plan. The lesson asks two questions. How can several servers agree to start one run? How can the incident lead tell when customers can use Payments again?

Oracle documents DR Protection Groups, plans, plan executions, prechecks, and execution history. The three voters, version 17, request IDs A and B, and execution DR-42 belong to the teaching example. Raft and Paxos are two alternative designs for the coordinator. The lesson makes no claim about which protocol OCI uses internally. All diagrams, sample replies, code, and incident traces illustrate that design.

## The OCI workflow

- **Where does the operator start?** Oracle's walkthrough shows how a primary group and a standby group work together. Its example explains where plans are created and shows how group roles change. Use it to connect the Ashburn and Phoenix roles to the console. [Oracle QuickDR walkthrough](https://docs.oracle.com/en/learn/full-stack-dr-quickvm-dr/)
- **What does starting a plan mean?** Oracle documents how to create a plan execution from the standby protection group and how to enable prechecks. Chapters 1 and 7 use that workflow. A new execution gives the incident team a run to track. [Create DR Plan Executions](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/disaster-recovery/doc/create-dr-plan-executions.html)
- **What runs together?** Plan groups run in order. Steps inside each group run in parallel. Prechecks validate plan elements without changing the topology. Chapters 7 and 8 use a Payments test transaction as a separate application check. [Overview of DR Plan Executions](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/disaster-recovery/doc/overview-dr-plan-executions.html)
- **Where should an operator look after an interruption?** The standby protection group's Plan Executions page lists runs and prechecks. Oracle distinguishes resuming a paused execution from retrying a step in a failed execution. Those actions apply to different states. [Resume a DR Plan Execution](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/disaster-recovery/doc/resume-dr-plan-executions.html) · [Retry a Step](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/disaster-recovery/doc/retry-dr-plan-executions.html)

## The protocol behind each exercise

| Lesson question | Source and what it establishes |
| --- | --- |
| Why can two of three voters carry a decision forward? | In a fixed group, any two majorities overlap. What the shared voter saved and the rules it follows matter. The Raft paper explains voting and log safety in sections 5.2–5.4. [Ongaro and Ousterhout, Raft](https://raft.github.io/raft.pdf) |
| What if a Raft leader dies after copying an entry? | Section 5.4.2 and Figure 8 explain why the entry's term matters. Section 8 discusses client retries and safe reads. The paper also explains membership changes and snapshots in sections 6 and 7. [Raft, extended version](https://raft.github.io/raft.pdf) |
| How does Paxos keep the original choice? | Section 2 follows prepare, promise, and accept. A new proposer uses the value with the highest accepted ballot reported in its prepare quorum, if any. A value can be chosen before the caller hears the result. [Leslie Lamport, Paxos Made Simple](https://lamport.azurewebsites.net/pubs/paxos-simple.pdf) |

## Connections to the five target companies

The questions below are original interview practice. Each uses the Payments incident. The linked publication or role explains why that skill matters.

- **OpenAI.** The engineering team describes operating Kubernetes at large scale. Practice explaining which events need agreement during an outage. Should every health probe take a vote, or only the decision to start the failover? OpenAI's public interview guide also discusses communication. [Engineering article](https://openai.com/index/scaling-kubernetes-to-7500-nodes/) · [Interview guide](https://openai.com/interview-guide/)
- **Anthropic.** Its published Staff+ distributed systems role covers infrastructure that teams depend on to train and serve models. Practice handing an incident to a second team. What can that team learn from DR-42, and what does it still need to test? [Staff+ infrastructure role](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/anthropic/jobs/4970314008-0)
- **Meta.** The Facebook Delos paper describes a shared log whose implementation can change. Practice replacing one Payments voter while keeping earlier decisions available to the next leader. [Virtual Consensus in Delos](https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi20/presentation/balakrishnan)
- **Google.** Google's Paxos engineers describe the practical problems of building a database around the protocol. Practice explaining how an operator recovers a result when the service saved the decision and the reply got lost. [Paxos Made Live](https://research.google/pubs/paxos-made-live-an-engineering-perspective-2006-invited-talk/)
- **NVIDIA.** The Megatron Bridge documentation describes mechanisms for recovering interrupted work. Practice the same reasoning with a Phoenix recovery step. What has the worker saved, and what evidence would make it safe to continue? [Megatron Bridge resiliency](https://docs.nvidia.com/nemo/megatron-bridge/latest/training/resiliency.html)
