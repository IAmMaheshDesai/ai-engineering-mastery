## Original Staff+ practice

Design a five-replica consensus-backed GPU allocation control plane. Explain what happens when the leader crashes after committing but before replying; when an old leader reaches a GPU worker after losing its quorum; and when an availability zone fails. Defend why fencing must be enforced by workers and why an operation ID is required for retry-safe external calls.
