## Foundational evidence and traceability

1. Maurice Herlihy and Jeannette Wing, *Linearizability: A Correctness Condition for Concurrent Objects*, ACM TOPLAS, 1990. Chapters 1–2 and 8 use the legal sequential explanation with precedence for nonoverlapping operations. The last-slot history and simple checker are original worked examples. https://www.cs.columbia.edu/~wing/publications/HerlihyWing90.pdf

2. Jepsen, *Sequential Consistency*, explanatory model reference, date unspecified. Chapter 2 contrasts per-process order with cross-process real-time order. https://jepsen.io/consistency/models/sequential

3. Douglas Terry, Alan Demers, Karin Petersen, Mike Spreitzer, Marvin Theimer and Brent Welch, *Session Guarantees for Weakly Consistent Replicated Data*, PDIS 1994. Chapter 3 distinguishes the four session guarantees. Revision tokens are an original proposed implementation for appropriately scoped replicated streams. https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/331722/

4. Wyatt Lloyd and colleagues, *Don't Settle for Eventual: Scalable Causal Consistency for Wide-Area Storage with COPS*, SOSP 2011. Chapter 4 draws on causal dependency enforcement and convergent conflict handling. Its two-origin vector illustration is independent of COPS's particular implementation. https://doi.org/10.1145/2043556.2043593

5. Hagit Attiya, Amotz Bar-Noy and Danny Dolev, *Sharing Memory Robustly in Message-Passing Systems*, JACM 1995. Chapter 6 uses the single-writer atomic-register setting to explain why quorum intersection needs an actual read protocol. The broken-register counterexample is illustrative. https://groups.csail.mit.edu/tds/papers/Attiya/JACM95.pdf

## OpenAI

6. Bohan Zhang, *Scaling PostgreSQL to power 800 million ChatGPT users*, January 22, 2026. The article describes a historical multi-region read-replica deployment and read-heavy workloads. Chapter 7 uses an original, smaller release registry to examine traffic isolation and the cost of routing reads to an authority. The design and capacity figures do not describe OpenAI's present storage architecture. https://openai.com/index/scaling-postgresql/

Original Staff+ practice. Specify consistency per endpoint, reserve capacity for freshness-constrained fallback during a regional outage and defend which calls may return a historical snapshot.

## Meta

## Google

## Corrections, assumptions and limitations

The lesson corrects the claims that every overlapping stale read violates linearizability, that eventual convergence guarantees a time bound, that a replica's highest received index is automatically safe to serve and that majority intersection alone implements an atomic register. No correction to supplied research was necessary. In the diagrams and worked cases, register histories use known initial values, explicit writers and labeled invocation intervals. Session floors assume comparable lineage and applied-prefix evidence. The miniature register checker covers completed operations only, explores permutations and is appropriate for small diagnostic histories. Production linearizability testing must also handle pending operations, indeterminate outcomes, clock uncertainty and tractable search bounds.
