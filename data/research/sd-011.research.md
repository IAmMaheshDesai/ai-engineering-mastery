## OpenAI — verified public engineering evidence

The 2021 Kubernetes account describes large multi-node jobs, disruption when participating pods fail, and restarting from checkpoints. It establishes a concrete historical connection between infrastructure reliability and useful training progress. It does not establish OpenAI’s current topology, recovery objectives, regional architecture, or interview rubric.

**Original practice relevance:** explain why preserving a running training data plane can matter more than immediate restoration of a management interface. Defend how admission, spare capacity, and checkpoint validity affect recovery. Senior Staff judgment means defining the customer or researcher outcome before selecting redundancy. No direct company-specific RPO/RTO commitment was verified.

## Anthropic — verified public engineering evidence and limits

The 2024 article distinguishes predefined workflows from agents whose model dynamically directs tool use. That supports the setting for studying durable agent actions. It does not document Anthropic’s internal disaster-recovery storage or failover protocol. Its tooling discussion is explicitly historical.

**Original practice relevance:** recover an executor after a tool accepted a request but before the executor recorded the response. Defend stable action identity, outcome lookup, authorization, and when to pause. This tests cross-service correctness rather than knowledge of a framework. No direct internal HA topology or numeric recovery objective was verified.

## Meta — verified public engineering evidence

The 2024 training article discusses increased interruption likelihood as GPU job size grows, fast recovery, preservation of training state, and adequate spare capacity. ## Google — verified public engineering evidence

The SRE book gives time-based availability examples, discusses testing as a way to reduce uncertainty, and treats overload as an operational design problem. These sources support the lesson’s availability definitions, drill discipline, and capacity-aware recovery boundary. They do not imply a particular current Google AI deployment or universal service SLO.

## NVIDIA — verified public engineering evidence

## Corrections, limitations, and unresolved questions

- No supplied research claim required correction. Important misconception corrections include timeout versus failure, replication versus historical backup, and endpoint health versus business recovery.
- Current numeric RPO/RTO commitments, private multi-region topology, and internal authority protocols at the five target companies were not established. The lesson makes no such claims.
- NVIDIA’s reference is archived; Anthropic’s article is historical and notes tooling changes. Version-specific behavior must be rechecked before operational use.
- The initial Oracle overview URL was not retrievable; the accessible dependency and cloud-adoption pages above were used instead.
- The checkpoint interval model assumes rare approximately uniform interruptions and blocking checkpoints; overlap, correlated failures, and partial restart require a different measured model.
- The independent-replica availability model is illustrative, not a forecast. Rates, costs, objectives, and topology are hypothetical unless explicitly attributed.
- Actual regional drills, GPU performance benchmarks, provider fencing, and production restore tests were not executed. Browser validation checks the learning artifact, not these architectures.
