## OpenAI — verified evidence and original practice

The published Kubernetes account provides historical fleet-management context. It does not specify a current private control-plane topology. Original practice: distinguish warm execution from scheduling new work, estimate management bursts, and defend a bootstrap path under realistic authority constraints.

## Anthropic — verified evidence and limits

The multi-agent article provides public context for orchestration and research workflows. It does not establish private delegated-authority or tool-gateway protocols. Original practice: separate orchestration state, scoped approval, tool execution, and unknown external effects. The lesson's capability model is a proposed design.

## Meta — public historical engineering

Twine describes a public cluster-management system and motivates attention to fleet allocation and operational boundaries. Original practice: explain why fleet management and workload execution have different objectives. No historical performance claim is turned into a present-day guarantee.

## Google — published research

Borg supplies cluster-management research context. Original practice: identify desired state, observed inventory, reconciliation, and shared dependency risks. Our ten-thousand-worker fleet, revision sequence, and capacity figures are invented teaching assumptions.

## NVIDIA — documented product lifecycle

Triton documents model-management modes and loading behavior. Original practice: separate model repository visibility, verified bundle preparation, activation, route readiness, and in-flight request pinning. This is not a claim that Triton alone implements the lesson's regional platform.

## Supporting sources and correctness boundaries

Kubernetes grounds component terminology; AWS motivates static stability; etcd documents its actual API guarantees. The lesson does not assume every store or watch has etcd semantics. Fencing must be enforced at the protected effect endpoint; idempotency and reconciliation remain separate requirements. Cached state can continue only while its authorization and compatibility are valid.
