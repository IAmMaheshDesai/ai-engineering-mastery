## Theory and evidence

1. Seth Gilbert and Nancy Lynch, *Brewer's Conjecture and the Feasibility of Consistent, Available, Partition-Tolerant Web Services*, SIGACT News, 2002. The asynchronous read/write register impossibility result grounds chapter 1. The model-release histories are a newly constructed instance of the proof argument. https://sites.cs.ucsb.edu/~rich/class/cs293b-cloud/papers/cap-proof.pdf

2. Seth Gilbert and Nancy Lynch, *Perspectives on the CAP Theorem*, IEEE Computer, 2012. This later treatment revisits the model and operational interpretation. Chapters 1–2 separate unbounded eventual successful completion, client deadlines, and which participants or traffic classes are affected. https://groups.csail.mit.edu/tds/papers/Gilbert/Brewer2.pdf

3. Daniel Abadi, *Consistency Tradeoffs in Modern Distributed Database System Design: CAP is Only Part of the Story*, IEEE Computer 45(2), 2012. PACELC draws attention to ordinary latency/consistency decisions as well as availability/consistency under a partition. Chapter 3 applies the lens to an original parallel replication trace. https://www.cs.umd.edu/~abadi/papers/abadi-pacelc.pdf

4. Giuseppe DeCandia and colleagues, *Dynamo: Amazon's Highly Available Key-value Store*, SOSP 2007. Historical example of designing a service for high availability and handling conflicting versions. Chapter 4's bounded quota counterexample and escrow allocation are independent exercises. Dynamo's historical choices do not establish current behavior of any cloud database product. https://www.amazon.science/publications/dynamo-amazons-highly-available-key-value-store

## Company-specific preparation

5. OpenAI, *Scaling Kubernetes to 7,500 nodes*, January 25, 2021. This published account discusses control-plane component load, observability and large research clusters. Original practice: assess whether one isolated regional API tier can truthfully report cluster-wide model readiness, and identify which local operations require only valid cached evidence. The lecture does not identify OpenAI's current deployment or revocation protocol. https://openai.com/index/scaling-kubernetes-to-7500-nodes/

6. Anthropic, *Staff+ Software Engineer, Infrastructure (Distributed Systems)*, public role description; publication date unspecified. The listing connects the role to large distributed systems supporting training and serving. Original practice: negotiate disconnected service, model withdrawal deadlines and observability with inference, reliability and platform teams. The role establishes job relevance without establishing a particular CAP interview question. https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/anthropic/jobs/4970314008-0

7. Meta Engineering, *Cache made consistent*, June 8, 2022. Meta discusses measuring client-observable cache inconsistency through Polaris. Chapter 7 uses this as motivation for measuring outcomes against the promised semantics. The example counts and deadlines are hypothetical and separate from Meta's measurements. Original practice: expose stale fallbacks hidden by healthy HTTP status rates. https://engineering.fb.com/2022/06/08/core-infra/cache-made-consistent/

8. James Corbett and colleagues, *Spanner: Google's Globally-Distributed Database*, OSDI 2012. The paper describes synchronous replication, external consistency and a time API that represents uncertainty. Original practice: distinguish the availability of a verified historical snapshot from a guarantee of the globally latest state, then articulate the assumptions permitting local service. The lesson's three-node numerical example is a generic hypothetical protocol. https://research.google/pubs/spanner-googles-globally-distributed-database-2/

9. NVIDIA, *Model Management*, Triton Inference Server user guide, versioned documentation with publication date unspecified. The guide documents distinct model-control modes and the relationship between repository changes and what a server loads. Chapter 5's split between publication and observed readiness is an original fleet-level design. Original practice: determine what a committed desired revision proves about the models installed in an isolated region. https://docs.nvidia.com/deeplearning/triton-inference-server/user-guide/docs/user_guide/model_management.html

## Traceability, clarifications and limitations

The failure-domain diagrams and sequence drawings are original inline SVG. Chapters 4–8 apply theory to invented systems. The OCI-style recovery exercise illustrates a hypothetical remote-log boundary without asserting the behavior of a particular provider or stating facts about the learner's experience. Verify the actual durability, replay, authorization and isolation contracts before applying this reasoning to a production service.

The lesson explicitly distinguishes CAP consistency from an arbitrary business invariant, successful availability from returning an error, and evidence of synchronization from a recent heartbeat. It also separates data recoverability from freshness and write authority. The inputs contained no empirical source claims requiring correction. No confidential company design, guaranteed interview format or measured benchmark is asserted.
