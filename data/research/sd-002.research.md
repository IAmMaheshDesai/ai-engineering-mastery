# Who may commit the next write?

**sd-002 · Research & Sources · Reviewed November 22, 2026**

A coordinator can decide who owns a shard while an old process still has a command in flight. Staff+ ownership design connects three decisions across team boundaries. Someone must issue an ordered grant, someone must enforce it at the consequential write, and someone must resolve effects already accepted when the caller disappeared. The lesson’s OCI-style failover and AI platform designs are hypothetical.

## OpenAI

**Verified engineering evidence.** OpenAI’s 2021 account of operating Kubernetes clusters at 7,500 nodes describes attention to API server and etcd load, dedicated control-plane machines and isolation of event traffic. It documents a particular deployment at publication time. [Scaling Kubernetes to 7,500 nodes](https://openai.com/index/scaling-kubernetes-to-7500-nodes/)

**Original interview practice.** A fleet restart causes thousands of scheduler leases to renew together. Defend jitter, priority for near-expiry renewals and a cap on retry concurrency. Demonstrate Staff+ capacity judgment by estimating peak demand and explaining how to keep grant traffic from starving durable mutations. The published cluster size is historical evidence, not a sizing assumption about the current fleet.

## Anthropic

**Verified engineering evidence.** Anthropic distinguishes predefined workflows from agents that dynamically choose steps and interact with tools. The engineering article emphasizes tool design and obtaining feedback from the environment. [Building effective agents, December 19, 2024](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents)

**Original interview practice.** Two executors resume one agent run after a timeout. Explain which state belongs in the workflow journal and which guarantee must come from a tool provider. Compare replaying an idempotent tool call with pausing a nonrepeatable action for reconciliation. The skill being practiced is designing a safe contract across systems with independent failure modes.

## Meta

**Verified engineering evidence.** Meta explains how larger distributed training jobs experience interruptions as GPU counts grow and discusses the operational challenges of keeping training productive. [How Meta trains large language models at scale, June 12, 2024](https://engineering.fb.com/2024/06/12/data-infrastructure/training-large-language-models-at-scale-meta/)

**Original interview practice.** A replacement scheduler sees stale placement records during a training run. Defend retaining healthy accepted reservations while quarantining unreachable nodes from new allocations. Quantify the cost of a gang restart and distinguish useful training throughput from scheduler readiness. The specific epoch and reservation design is an exercise.

## Google

**Verified engineering evidence.** Burrows describes Chubby’s coarse-grained advisory locks and the sequencer mechanism by which clients can present evidence of lock state to a cooperating recipient. This source directly addresses delayed requests from former lock holders. [The Chubby lock service for loosely-coupled distributed systems, OSDI 2006](https://research.google/pubs/the-chubby-lock-service-for-loosely-coupled-distributed-systems/)

**Original interview practice.** Compare installing a durable floor at a checkpoint manifest service with consulting an authority for each write. Draw the race between checking a grant and committing the manifest. Defend an atomic conditional write and discuss the cost of making the coordination service part of the data path.

## NVIDIA

**Verified engineering evidence.** NVIDIA documents local checkpointing, optional replication across nodes, and loading a valid checkpoint when recovering. The source establishes the recovery problem and storage choices. [NeMo Framework resiliency features, documentation release 25.07](https://docs.nvidia.com/nemo-framework/user-guide/25.07/resiliency.html)

**Original interview practice.** A paused coordinator finishes uploading checkpoint shards after its successor has started a new attempt. Allow immutable data uploads, but condition publication on the current run incarnation, writer epoch, completeness, and expected step. Defend the cost of orphan cleanup against the throughput impact of putting all tensor writes through one synchronous coordinator.

## Protocol references

- Ongaro and Ousterhout explain Raft terms, elections and quorum intersection. An application ownership epoch can advance while the Raft term stays the same. [In Search of an Understandable Consensus Algorithm, 2014](https://web.stanford.edu/~ouster/cgi-bin/papers/raft-extended.pdf)
- The etcd specification distinguishes key-value transaction guarantees from asynchronous watch delivery and describes leases. Use watched state as a notification; use the documented conditional operation for an ownership claim. [etcd API guarantees, release 3.6](https://etcd.io/docs/v3.6/learning/api_guarantees/)
- The etcd project’s worked example illustrates how an expired lock holder can still act and motivates destination-side validation. [etcd distributed locking example, living repository](https://github.com/etcd-io/etcd/blob/main/contrib/lock/README.md)
