## Technical caution

A monotonic fencing token protects a resource only if the resource or authoritative gateway enforces the generation atomically and durably. Establishing a new epoch does not cancel already accepted external work. Lease safety depends on the specified timing and protocol assumptions. Neither leader election nor fencing alone guarantees exactly-once external effects.
